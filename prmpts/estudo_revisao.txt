Pergunta: What is the effect of the 'final' keyword on a variable?

Código-Fonte:
  01 | final double PI = 3.14159;
  02 | // PI = 3.14; // ?

Alternativas:
  [A] The variable is deleted by garbage collection immediately. [SUA ESCOLHA]
  [B] The variable can only be assigned once and cannot be changed. [CORRETA]
  [C] The variable is converted to a String.
  [D] The variable becomes private to the class.

Explicação Oficial:
  The 'final' keyword prevents reassignment. Once initialized, its value cannot be modified.
Referência da Especificação Oficial:
  JLS §4.12.4 (final Variables)
